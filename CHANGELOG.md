# Changelog

## v1.2.0 — [HIGH HEELS (예뻐지게)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0yAN3zJQPUg)

- Link colors rebuilt: saved and Firefox-history links are colored reliably, with a live preview and a list of excluded sites.
- Tags: suggestions as you type, quick-tag buttons, and tools to rename, merge or delete a tag and to tag several pages at once.
- Search: filter by tag, site, date, untagged or untitled pages, in the Dashboard and the sidebar.
- Stats: hover the activity chart for details, and click a tag, site or day to open those pages.
- Default shortcuts: Alt+Shift+V (toggle), M (mark) and U (unmark). The sidebar now opens in your theme right away.
- Version bump from Firefox 128 to Firefox 140 or newer.

## v1.1.0 — [LIKE (궁금해)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hhlU34DloDY)

- Improved saved-link detection and highlighting across different websites.
- Added experimental automatic and manual lookup for missing page titles.
- Added keyboard shortcuts to mark, unmark, or toggle the current page.
- Simplified and improved JSON/CSV exports.
- Fixed theme color inconsistencies throughout the Dashboard, including Stats charts.

## v1.0.0 — [PEPE (페페)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3qmsJbJasfo)

Initial stable release.
